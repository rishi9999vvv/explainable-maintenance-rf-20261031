MEASURED CONCEPTS

What changed in the signal?

Channel-averaged summaries from individual captures. Gaps between captures are not continuous recordings.

TREE SHAP EXPLANATION

What drove the forest’s score?

Signed TreeSHAP contributions. Positive values raise the score; negative values lower it.

Contributions are averaged over the same past scoring windows as the displayed score. They reconstruct that score and explain the model, not physical causation.
